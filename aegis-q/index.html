Trust & authority boundaries

AEGIS-Q

A simple security principle with serious consequences: an AI may request an action, but it must never grant itself the authority to perform it.

The core rule

Model-generated intent and trusted authority are separate. Genesis can reason about an action and request it; a separate policy and authorization layer decides whether it is permitted.

What the foundation is exploring

  • Trusted policy gates for consequential actions.
  • Explicit authority boundaries and revocation.
  • Validation of edge cases, malformed inputs, and uncertain states.
  • Auditable decisions that do not depend on the model declaring itself trusted.

Evidence & status

AEGIS-Q work is being developed within the Sovereign ecosystem. The authority foundation has been represented in draft pull-request work; a draft is not the same as a merged release or independent security certification.

Review Sovereign-Core pull requests →

The design objective is to make authority enforceable by architecture and testable behavior, not by promises in a system prompt.
