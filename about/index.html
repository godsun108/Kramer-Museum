The person behind the projects

Curious by nature. Driven to build.

I’m interested in the space where creativity, technology, practical problem-solving, and care for the natural world meet.

This is a personal home base: part introduction, part working portfolio, part record of ideas becoming tangible projects.

How I approach things

I like learning by building, testing assumptions, and improving what exists. I’m drawn to independent systems, useful tools, original creative work, and projects that can contribute something positive beyond themselves.

I value curiosity, resilience, personal responsibility, and treating people well. A simple principle matters more than a polished slogan: just be nice.
