EarthChain & TruFusion

Exploring the foundations for connected digital experiences, with attention to identity, durable records, and accountable system behavior.

Direction

The broader vision connects digital identity, wallet experiences, creator tools, exchange concepts, community participation, and interactive applications.

Engineering priorities

  • Reliable persistence and recovery under interruption.
  • Independent consensus and cross-node behavior.
  • Device testing and clear operational boundaries.
  • Security and authority controls integrated with the wider Sovereign ecosystem.

Current status

These are developing foundations and prototypes. Work on persistence or consensus is not a claim of a launched mainnet, audited financial infrastructure, or production-ready exchange.
