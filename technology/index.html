Technology & systems

Build systems that earn trust.

Independent exploration of AI, software infrastructure, digital identity, and security—with a focus on useful capabilities and clear authority boundaries.

The technical work centers on building systems that can reason and assist while keeping consequential authority accountable, bounded, and outside the model itself.

In development

Genesis

An ambitious assistant and AI system intended to connect reasoning, tools, and continuity across experiences. The long-term direction includes private and device-local operation; that ambition should not be confused with a completed on-device product.

Security foundation

AEGIS-Q

Authority and policy foundations for agentic systems. A core design principle: an AI may request an action, but must not grant itself permission to perform consequential actions.

Developing foundations

EarthChain

Work on persistence, identity, wallet experiences, and distributed-system foundations. Engineering milestones are not a claim of mainnet readiness or financial certification.

Engineering principles

  • Human and policy authority remains separate from model-generated intent.
  • Security claims should be supported by tests and review, not branding.
  • Offline capability and device independence are goals to validate incrementally.
  • Document what passed, what remains draft, and what has not been verified.

The repositories are the best place to inspect current source, pull requests, and test evidence. Project status can change; this page is a high-level overview, not a certification statement.
